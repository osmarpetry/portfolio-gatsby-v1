This blog begin with a idea to I have a journal. But not a personal journal, a public journal. Where I post everything I'm doing, superficially.

I have a big difficulty to speak lot of words in a text, but I give me a try. I should take care to write something that make sense to the others, because of that I will keep my texts simple.

Intension of this blog is to work as my journal, but also training me to write more and better. My conclusion Thesis on university I had lot of difficulty to write the pages (with LaTex), and I want to do a master degree on Applied Computing because of some friends and colleges, for that reason I must write very well.

But let me talk about some inspirations.

### Lucas Costa - [https://lucasfcosta.com/](lucascosta.com)

I know Lucas Costa from my first TDC on Florianópolis, he probably don't know me. I tried to keep this blog stupid simple, for maintain and for read, like he did.

My blog got a lot of inspirations from his blog. I have a very similar header, footer, about-me and message above my name, on the title of my blog.

I hope have so awesome content like that he have, on my blog. He is also one of my inspiration, because of his blog and his open-source contributions.

### Kent C. Dodds - [https://kentcdodds.com/](kentcdodds.com)

I was trying to find a best color for my blog, something different. But I found his color the best, so I tried the same hex color of the blue color of Brazilian flag. But after a talk to my girlfriend, she convince  me to replace white with blue, to a dark with dim theme as default.

Something very similar is the blog list, very simple and clear.

I love his content about tests, he did the unit test something very simple and useful. Focus on final result, instead of take multiple hours to test avery corner of the implementation code (like state on React).

### William Justen - [https://willianjusten.com.br/](https://willianjusten.com.br/)

This case is not the blog visual, but his course on Udemy. Help me understand better how to work with Gatsby to have a simple blog implementation.

My first try was create a blog with a lot of features, like he did. But after some work hours, the blog look ugly and the code was a nightmare. But with the beta blog, I learn how to not code to Gatsby and delivery something simple. Now I have a simple blog, but I blog that works.
